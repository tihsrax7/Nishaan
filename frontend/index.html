Every copy carries its reader's mark. Every opening is signed, voted on and chained.

  1. 01A document is encrypted once and its key is split across five ledger nodes, so no single server holds it.
  2. 02An officer signs a post-quantum receipt to open it. Four of five nodes must verify and co-sign.

  4. 04If it leaks, the mark names the officer, and the ledger shows when they opened it.

    Documents

    Opening a document runs the full NISHAAN protocol. You sign a receipt, the ledger nodes check it and vote, three nodes release their key shares, and the rebuilt key is sent to you wrapped under your own ML-KEM key. You then get a copy carrying your personal mark.

    Request access

      Every successful opening becomes a block: the officer's signed receipt, sealed with a SHA3-256 hash, linked to the block before it, and co-signed by at least four nodes. Verify re-checks every hash, link and signature from scratch.
